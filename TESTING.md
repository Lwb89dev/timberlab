# Testing

Two runners cover the same foundation:

- `build/timberlab-tests` — Catch2, 95 test cases. These do not construct the Qt controller.
- `build/timberlab --check` — the same model checks linked into the application, without Qt
- `build/timberlab --accept` — the editor controller, without a window. It creates two boards, types exact sizes, saves, reopens, edits again, and checks nanometres plus the rebuilt mesh. It then adds a mortise and tenon, shortens the tenon, undoes that edit, and reloads the cut.

On the baseline that introduced this file, Catch2 reported 466 assertions and `--check` printed `self-check ok`. After the editor acceptance path and the box-mesh vertex check were added, Catch2 reported 472 assertions in 21 cases. After the debug pass (save backup, size and text limits, preference error codes), Catch2 reported 512 assertions in 25 cases. After the first joinery set, Catch2 reported 623 assertions in 30 cases, `--check` printed `self-check ok`, and `--accept` printed `accept ok` for an 800 × 19.05 × 400 mm shelf with a mortise and a 24 × 3/4 × 12 inch rail with a tenon, reloaded from `build/accept/boards.timberlab`. After parameter propagation, Catch2 reported 733 assertions in 40 cases. `--check` printed `self-check ok`, including a demo width of 1500 mm (top 1500 mm, front apron 1300 mm, leg 2 at X 1400 mm) and the undo back to 1200 mm, 1000 mm, and 1100 mm. `--accept` again printed `accept ok` for the same unbound shelf and rail: those two boards have no parameters. After the debug pass that rejects an edit when a stored joint would no longer fit, Catch2 reported 766 assertions in 42 cases. `--check` printed `self-check ok` and `--accept` printed `accept ok` again. After the user library, Catch2 reported 2855 assertions in 47 cases. Most of the new assertions are the 2000-row cap, one per inserted material. `--check` printed `self-check ok`, including a library material and an 18 mm preset reopened from `library.sqlite`. `--accept` printed `accept ok` again, and also saved a user material onto the shelf, reloaded that id, and left the mortise in place. Adding the preset did not change the shelf thickness. `--accept` forces millimetres after loading preferences, so a previous inch setting in that data directory does not change the metric checks. After assemblies, Catch2 reported 3161 assertions in 57 test cases. `--check` printed `self-check ok`, including a carcass whose width edit to 900 mm moved the right side by 300 mm, then undo put it back. `--accept` printed `accept ok` again for the same unbound shelf and rail. `lrelease` reported 204 finished translations and 0 unfinished. After the parent link, the five extra joints, the frame, shelving, the bedside table, the cut list, and the local cipher, Catch2 reported 3406 assertions in 76 test cases. `--check` printed `self-check ok`, including the frame stile at X 540 mm. `--accept` printed `accept ok` again for the same unbound shelf and rail. `lrelease` reported 252 finished translations and 0 unfinished. After the orthographic wires, the library envelope, the portable length divide, and the explicit backup restore, Catch2 reported 3506 assertions in 80 test cases. `--check` printed `self-check ok`. `--accept` printed `accept ok` again for the same unbound shelf and rail, reloaded from `build/accept/boards.timberlab`. `lrelease` reported 278 finished translations and 0 unfinished. After Nostr sign-in, Catch2 reported 3755 assertions in 89 test cases. The new cases lock the published NIP-19, BIP-340, NIP-44, and NIP-01 vectors and the bunker state machine. They do not open a socket. `--check` printed `self-check ok`, including that nsec, the matching npub, the short NIP-44 payload, and an Amber permission list that does not ask to sign events. `--accept` printed `accept ok` again for the same unbound shelf and rail, reloaded from `build/accept/boards.timberlab`. `lrelease` reported 301 finished translations and 0 unfinished. After the finger joint, the half-blind dovetail, tongue and groove, the dowel, the front overall dimensions, and the kind 30078 library event, Catch2 reported 3897 assertions in 95 test cases. `--check` printed `self-check ok`, including a kind 30078 round trip of a short library tag. `--accept` printed `accept ok` again for the same unbound shelf and rail, reloaded from `build/accept/boards.timberlab`. `lrelease` reported 321 finished translations and 0 unfinished. The new cases do not open a socket. Amber was not run on a device. A window was started with `--demo` on this machine and left running until the timeout; the log was empty. The new menu items were not clicked. Re-run them after you change units, documents, solids, storage, or the controller. A green result in a later edit is not implied by this paragraph.

`TIMBERLAB_TEST_DIR` for Catch2 is the build directory `testdata/`. `--check` writes under `TIMBERLAB_DATA_DIR` or, if that is unset, `build/self-check`. Set the variable so a test does not touch `~/.local/share`.

## What is asserted

- Exact nanometres for the imperial preset list from 1/8 inch to 4 inch, and a round trip through the formatter
- 64 × 1/64 inch equals 1 inch
- `3' 6 1/4"` and `1,5 cm`
- An inexact inch decimal parses back to the same nanometres
- Display unit changes do not rewrite a stored thickness of 3/4 inch (19 050 000 nm, shown as `19.05 mm`)
- Undo of a unit change leaves the thickness; a second undo restores the previous thickness
- Lock blocks a dimension edit and still allows hide. A duplicate is unlocked
- JSON round trip, rejected malformed JSON, rejected overlong names and notes, rejected non-finite or huge angles, rejected duplicate ids, rejected more than 2000 components
- The demo table has 9 parts whose world boxes touch and do not overlap. Setting `table.width` to 1500 mm sets the top to 1500 mm, the front apron to 1300 mm, and leg 2's X to 1400 mm, still without overlap. Undo restores 1200 mm, 1000 mm, and 1100 mm. A typed edit of the bound top width is `parameter.driven`. A width that collapses an apron rolls the document back
- Shrinking a mortised shelf from 600 mm to 10 mm is rejected (`joint.parameter`) and the document is unchanged, whether the width is typed or driven. A width of 400 mm still fits and reloads. A locked part refuses a new binding and a release. A parameter that already drives that part still moves it
- A new library file is schema version 3, and a new row has revision 1. A planted version 1 file migrates, keeps the material and the 18 mm preset, and then accepts another row. A planted version 2 file gains revision 1 and keeps its notes. A version 4 file is refused and its row stays. Opening a preferences file as a library fails with `library.schema` and the preference stays. A bad name, color, thickness, or duplicate id is rejected. The 2001st material is `library.too_many`
- A 90 degree Z rotation sends local +X to world +Y
- Box 100 × 18 × 200 mm volume and bounds, cylinder volume against π r² h, fine tessellation has more triangles than draft
- Zip save/load, a second save leaves the new file and a backup of the previous one, garbage file rejected, bad SHA-256 rejected, a file over 32 MiB rejected, raw JSON load, SQLite preference round trip, a preferences path that cannot be opened reports `preferences.open`. Restoring a garbage backup is `storage.format` and leaves the project bytes and the caller's document unchanged. A missing backup is `storage.restore`. Loading a damaged project does not copy the backup over it. After a valid backup is restored, the reloaded name is the backup's name
- At least 40 hardwood/softwood rows, a binomial name when the species is not marked ambiguous, no density, no Janka number
- A 2×4 nominal thickness differs from the surfaced thickness
- The dependency graph reports three dependents of `width`, orders `width` before `apron` before `tenon`, and detects the cycle `width → apron → tenon → width`
- Mortise and tenon cut both solids; a later thickness change recenters the tenon. Half-lap cuts both parts. Dado, stopped groove, and rabbet cut the host and leave the mate whole. A cylinder that no joint cuts stays a cylinder. Undo, a locked part, a joint that does not fit, and deleting a referenced part are covered. One meshed joint matches the probed volume
- An inserted table has 9 parts, 9 parameters, and 48 bindings. Its driver ids start with `table_` and are not `table.width`. The demo table still uses `table.width` and the stored name Width. Width 1500 mm updates the top, the front apron, and the far legs. Undo restores them. A typed edit of the bound top is `parameter.driven`; after release, a typed width sticks
- A carcass has 5 boards that touch and do not overlap. Width 900 mm moves the right side from 582 mm to 882 mm and widens the panels. Width 30 mm is `parameter.range` and leaves the document unchanged. The saved JSON has no `assemblies` array, and the same edit still moves the right side after reload
- A drawer box has 5 boards that touch and do not overlap. Height 200 mm grows the sides and the front; the bottom stays 6 mm thick. That thickness is `parameter.driven` until it is released
- A carcass placed after a table starts at X 1220 mm. Widening the table to 1500 mm does not move it. A table placed after a 600 mm board starts with leg 1 at X 660 mm. Two tables keep separate drivers. An unknown kind is `assembly.unknown` and leaves the document unchanged. A board rotated 90 degrees about Z does not reserve its unrotated width. An existing mortise stays when a drawer is added. 1996 parts reject a carcass with `storage.too_many`; 1995 parts accept it, and the project then has 2000 parts
- `--check` adds a carcass on a fresh document, sets its width to 900 mm, and checks that the right side moves by 300 mm. Undo restores that position. It also adds a frame and checks that the right stile is at X 540 mm, with role `stile` and an empty parent
- A bridle removes a centered slot from one board and both cheeks from the other. A three-finger castle removes two bands from part A and three from part B. A miter of 20 mm on a 20 mm edge removes half of that rectangle, and a point above the diagonal is outside the solid. A two-tail dovetail removes 5600 mm³ from the tails and 4000 mm³ from the pins. A sliding dovetail removes 2880 mm³ from the host and 1920 mm³ from the tongue. A four-band finger joint removes 9000 mm³ from each of two 80 × 40 × 30 mm boards. A half-blind dovetail with the same sizes as that two-tail dovetail removes the same 5600 mm³ and 4000 mm³, a point on the pin board's far side stays inside the solid, and a depth equal to the pin normal is refused while a through dovetail still accepts it. A tongue and groove removes 2400 mm³ from the host and 2400 mm³ from the tongue. A dowel of diameter 8 mm and depth 15 mm removes 240π mm³ from each board, and a diameter that is an odd number of nanometres is refused. Starting sizes from Add joint fit two 600 × 18 × 720 mm boards for bridle, castle, miter, both dovetails, the sliding dovetail, finger, half-blind, tongue and groove, and dowel, and both volumes drop. Castle reloads its count of 3. A castle, finger, or half-blind file with no count is `storage.json`
- A frame has four touching boards. Width 900 mm moves the right stile to X 840 mm and the rail to 780 mm. Undo restores 540 mm. Shelving is six boards. The side height is four shelf thicknesses plus three bays (972 mm at the defaults). Bay 400 mm makes that height 1272 mm and does not add a board. A bedside table has eleven boards. The drawer starts at Z 350 mm. Height 600 mm moves it to Z 400 mm. The lower shelf stays at Z 120 mm and 18 mm thick. Roles are set. `parentId` stays empty
- A parent link round-trips. The same parent again does not change the file. A self parent, a two-part loop, and a missing parent are rejected. A locked part refuses a parent edit. Deleting the parent clears the link on a locked child. Load rejects a missing parent and a cycle. A role token round-trips. `Leg` is `component.role`. An empty role is omitted from the JSON
- A cut list with allowance zero keeps rough equal to finished. Allowance 2 mm adds 2 mm on each reported edge and leaves the finished size. A negative allowance is `parameter.range`. A cylinder is a diameter in both width and thickness. Two matching rails become one bill-of-materials line with quantity 2. The JSON holds both lists. The front-view SVG of a 100 × 18 × 200 mm board is a wire whose view box is 102 by 202 mm, including a 1 mm margin, and a dado makes a longer path. The top view box is 102 by 20 mm. The right view box is 20 by 202 mm. The sheet is 132 by 232 mm, with the right view translated by 112 mm and the top view by 212 mm. The DXF is R12 and contains the front bottom edge from (0, 0) to (100, 0), a top edge from (0, 0) to (0, 18), and a right edge from (0, 0) to (0, 200), on layers FRONT, TOP, and RIGHT. The PDF starts with `%PDF-1.4`, ends at a real xref, names itself a tessellation wire, and draws that same front edge. The dimensioned front of that board says `100 mm` and `200 mm`, and its width line runs from 1 mm to 101 mm. A dado leaves those two texts unchanged. A cylinder is `dimension.not_applicable`
- AES-256-GCM round-trips a string, including an embedded NUL. The sealed bytes are longer by 28 and do not contain the plaintext. A flipped tag, a short buffer, or the wrong key is `sync.crypto` and yields no plaintext. Equal hashes keep local. The same revision with a different hash is `sync.conflict`. The higher revision wins. A negative revision is `sync.range`. A different id or kind is `sync.mismatch`. A sealed material at revision 2 replaces the library notes. Importing it again keeps local. The same revision with different notes is `sync.conflict` and leaves the notes. The wrong key is `sync.crypto`. A sealed preset at revision 4 updates the label. Through all of those imports the project file bytes stay the same. A kind 30078 event sealed to the published test nsec opens back to the same plaintext. A kind 1 event, a second key, an Amber session, and a signed-out session cannot open or seal it. A JSON array of one event imports through `importPlaintext`. Revision 2 replaces the notes. The same revision with different notes is `sync.conflict`. The project file bytes stay the same. The tests do not open a socket

The toolbar showed Assieme between Cilindro and Duplica. The inspector listed the nine demo parameters with the short Italian labels. The QML log was empty. The Assembly menu was not opened. Hide, viewport pick, Drive, Release, Add material, Add preset, and Remove were not clicked. That is a manual check, not a Catch2 case. A later `--demo` on `DISPLAY=:1` was left up for 12 seconds and the log stayed empty, which shows the QML loaded. The parent combo, the role field, the joint type list, the export dialogs, and Restore backup were not clicked. After the sign-in menu was added, `--demo` on `DISPLAY=:1` was left up for 12 seconds and the log stayed empty. The Account menu, the secret-key dialog, and the bunker dialog were not clicked. After the later joints and the library-event menu, `--demo` on `DISPLAY=:1` was left up for 12 seconds and the log stayed empty.
