# Testing

Two runners cover the same foundation:

- `build/timberlab-tests` — Catch2, 95 test cases. These do not construct the Qt controller.
- `build/timberlab --check` — the same model checks linked into the application, without Qt
- `build/timberlab --accept` — the editor controller, without a window. It creates two boards, types exact sizes, saves, reopens, edits again, and checks nanometres plus the rebuilt mesh. It then adds a mortise and tenon, shortens the tenon, undoes that edit, and reloads the cut.

On the baseline that introduced this file, Catch2 reported 466 assertions and `--check` printed `self-check ok`. After the editor acceptance path and the box-mesh vertex check were added, Catch2 reported 472 assertions in 21 cases. After the debug pass (save backup, size and text limits, preference error codes), Catch2 reported 512 assertions in 25 cases. After the user library, Catch2 reported 2855 assertions in 47 cases. `TIMBERLAB_TEST_DIR` for Catch2 is the build directory `testdata/`. `--check` writes under `TIMBERLAB_DATA_DIR` or, if that is unset, `build/self-check`. Set the variable so a test does not touch `~/.local/share`.

## What is asserted

- Exact nanometres for the imperial preset list from 1/8 inch to 4 inch, and a round trip through the formatter
- 64 × 1/64 inch equals 1 inch
- `3' 6 1/4"` and `1,5 cm`
- An inexact inch decimal parses back to the same nanometres
- Display unit changes do not rewrite a stored thickness of 3/4 inch (19 050 000 nm, shown as `19.05 mm`)
- Undo of a unit change leaves the thickness; a second undo restores the previous thickness
- Lock blocks a dimension edit and still allows hide. A duplicate is unlocked
- JSON round trip, rejected malformed JSON, rejected overlong names and notes, rejected non-finite or huge angles, rejected duplicate ids, rejected more than 2000 components
- The demo table has 9 parts whose world boxes touch and do not overlap. Setting `table.width` to 1500 mm sets the top to 1500 mm, the front apron to 1300 mm, and leg 2's X to 1400 mm, still without overlap. Undo restores 1200 mm, 1000 mm, and 1100 mm. - Shrinking a mortised shelf from 600 mm to 10 mm is rejected (`joint.parameter`) and the document is unchanged, whether the width is typed or driven. A width of 400 mm still fits and reloads. A locked part refuses a new binding and a release. A parameter that already drives that part still moves it
- A new library file is schema version 3, and a new row has revision 1. A planted version 1 file migrates, keeps the material and the 18 mm preset, and then accepts another row. A planted version 2 file gains revision 1 and keeps its notes. A version 4 file is refused and its row stays. Opening a preferences file as a library fails with `library.schema` and the preference stays. A bad name, color, thickness, or duplicate id is rejected. The 2001st material is `library.too_many`
- A 90 degree Z rotation sends local +X to world +Y
- Box 100 × 18 × 200 mm volume and bounds, cylinder volume against π r² h, fine tessellation has more triangles than draft
- Zip save/load, a second save leaves the new file and a backup of the previous one, garbage file rejected, bad SHA-256 rejected, a file over 32 MiB rejected, raw JSON load, SQLite preference round trip, a preferences path that cannot be opened reports `preferences.open`. - At least 40 hardwood/softwood rows, a binomial name when the species is not marked ambiguous, no density, no Janka number
- A 2×4 nominal thickness differs from the surfaced thickness
- The dependency graph reports three dependents of `width`, orders `width` before `apron` before `tenon`, and detects the cycle `width → apron → tenon → width`
- Mortise and tenon cut both solids; a later thickness change recenters the tenon. Half-lap cuts both parts. Dado, stopped groove, and rabbet cut the host and leave the mate whole. A cylinder that no joint cuts stays a cylinder. Undo, a locked part, a joint that does not fit, and deleting a referenced part are covered. One meshed joint matches the probed volume
- An inserted table has 9 parts, 9 parameters, and 48 bindings. Its driver ids start with `table_` and are not `table.width`. The demo table still uses `table.width` and the stored name Width. - A carcass placed after a table starts at X 1220 mm. Widening the table to 1500 mm does not move it. A table placed after a 600 mm board starts with leg 1 at X 660 mm. Two tables keep separate drivers. An unknown kind is `assembly.unknown` and leaves the document unchanged. A board rotated 90 degrees about Z does not reserve its unrotated width. ## The later joints, the Joinery button, and the new platforms

After the eight later joints (haunched, wedged, and loose tenons, cross lap, end lap, splined miter, biscuit, pocket screw) and the Joinery button, Catch2 reported 18371 assertions in 127 test cases, `--check` printed `self-check ok`, and `--accept` printed `accept ok`. The new volumes are computed by hand and compared within 1 mm³. The haunched tenon removes 8160 mm³ from a 40 × 18 × 40 mm tenon and 2208 mm³ from the mortise. A wedged tenon with one kerf removes 96 mm³ more than the plain tenon, and two kerfs 192 mm³. Two loose-tenon pockets remove 1920 mm³ from each part. A biscuit removes four times the circular segment of a 100 mm disc plunged 10 mm. A cross lap and an end lap remove the slots of their stated widths. A splined miter removes the miter triangle plus a kerf that is clipped by the board's outer face, `4 · (120√2 − 18)` mm³. A pocket screw removes `π · (16² + 4 · (34 / cos 15° − 16))` mm³ from its own part and nothing from the mate. Points on the axis of the angled hole are outside the solid and points just off the pilot's radius are inside. Starting sizes fit two default boards for six of the types. The cross lap and the end lap need boards whose V extent is shorter than the other board's length, so their starting sizes are checked with 200 and 300 mm boards. A file written before `length2` and `width2` existed still loads, and a wedged tenon with no `length2`, `width2`, or `count` is `storage.json`. `--accept` selects two touching boards, checks that the menu offers the haunched, wedged, and loose tenon, refuses a pair that is apart or a single board, and adds a joint to the pair.

`--demo` was started once with an offscreen platform and a screenshot of the toolbar was taken. The Joinery button showed disabled with nothing selected. The viewport is blank under that platform. The button was not clicked in a window, and the menu was not opened by hand. The new inspector fields were not looked at.

`packaging/build-android.sh` built a signed arm64 APK. The check was structural: `apksigner verify`, `aapt dump badging` (package, version 401, minimum SDK 28, target SDK 34, `arm64-v8a`), and a listing of the libraries in the archive, with the `NEEDED` entries of the application library. It was not installed or run, and no emulator was started. `.github/workflows/windows.yml` parsed as YAML. It has not run, and nothing was compiled with MSVC. The Linux build and tests were rerun after the Windows changes and passed.

## Hidden-line sheets, STL, STEP, the mesh cache, and the measurements

Catch2 reported 18430 assertions in 132 test cases, `--check` printed `self-check ok`, and `--accept` printed `accept ok`. The model tests were also built with AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer (`build/asan`, leak detection off because of OCCT) and passed with the same counts.

A slot cut into the face that faces the viewer shows no hidden edge in the front view. The same slot on the far face shows eight. Hidden edges that lie under visible ones are dropped, including a whole back edge that the visible edge covers in pieces. The sheet draws visible lines in one group and hidden lines dashed in another, and chains the dimensions 20, 40, 100, and 5 mm for a 100 × 20 × 40 mm board with a 20 × 5 mm slot. A mortise and tenon's tenon length, the board's 300 mm width, and its 60 mm height all appear as dimension text. A cylinder is drawn without dimensions. The DXF has the layers `VISIBLE`, `HIDDEN`, and `DIMENSIONS` and a dashed line type, and the PDF has a dash operator. The sheet was rendered once with Qt's SVG renderer and read by eye: three views, dashed hidden lines, dimension chains with the text rotated beside the vertical ones. The DXF and PDF were not opened in a CAD program or a viewer.

A 10 × 20 × 30 mm box is 12 triangles and 684 bytes of binary STL, spans 0 to 10 mm in X, and moves to 100 mm with a placement. A hidden part is left out of the project export, and a missing part id is refused. The STEP file of two named boards and a dowel holds two `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` entities, both names, a `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE`, and millimetre units. A single part id gives one solid. No STEP or STL file was opened in another CAD program.

`--accept` joins two touching boards, writes the STL, the STEP, and the hidden-line sheet of the pair, and after every edit (the mate's width, four joint sizes, and an undo) compares the volume of each part's mesh in the scene with the volume OCCT computes for the solid, within 0.1 percent. That is the check on the mesh cache: a part that kept an old tessellation would fail it.

`timberlab --bench` and `TIMBERLAB_FRAME_BENCH=<seconds>` produced the numbers in the README. The frame run opened a window under X11 on this machine's display for five seconds per scene. It was not run on a tablet, Windows, or macOS.

After the fixes in commit 5061e79, manual runs of `windows.yml` and `android.yml` on `main` both passed every step. The Windows run covered the Catch2 suite, `--check`, `--accept`, and `--check` and `--accept` from the package with only the Windows folders on the path. No window was opened. `macos.yml` parsed as YAML and has not run.

The release APK was built from scratch and signed with the release key. `apksigner verify` accepted it and printed the certificate digest that the keystore reports. `aapt dump badging` shows version code 500, minimum SDK 28, target SDK 34, `arm64-v8a`, and no debuggable flag.
